Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Steady LMS ("we", "us") is operated by Jainam Shah in San Jose, California. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. In short: we collect only what we need to talk to you and do the work, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • When you email us or book a call: your name, email address, organization and whatever you choose to tell us.
  • When you pay: your name, email and billing address. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card or bank account number.
  • When you visit this website: anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, rough location by country, browser type) through Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across other websites.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule calls and provide the services you buy.
  • To send invoices, receipts and service updates.
  • To understand which pages of this website are useful.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business: Google Workspace (email and calendar), Stripe (payments) and Cloudflare (website statistics). We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Your clients' and learners' data

When we work inside your LMS, learner data stays in your systems. We access only what the work needs, keep it confidential, and remove our access when the work ends. Our Terms of Service and any agreement we sign with you cover this in more detail.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and billing records as long as needed to serve you and to meet tax and accounting rules, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop emailing you, at any time. If you got an email from us and don't want more, just reply "no thanks". California residents have additional rights under California law, which we honor.
