Questions

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Care plans have a 3-month minimum so we can get your LMS into good shape, then they run month to month. You can cancel with 30 days' notice.

What if we need more hours one month?

We tell you before we go over. Extra hours are $110/hour and only happen with your OK. If you keep needing more, we'll suggest the next plan up.

How do you get into our LMS? Is our data safe?

We use our own named admin accounts with multi-factor sign-in, never a shared password, and only the access the work needs. Learner data stays in your systems, and we're happy to sign your NDA.

Can we start with a one-time project instead?

Yes. Many clients start with a project, such as fixing broken courses or building a report, and move to a care plan later.

How do we pay?

Care plans are paid online by card or US bank account and renew monthly. Projects are invoiced: half to start, half on delivery. Every payment gets an emailed receipt.

Our LMS isn't listed. Can you still help?

Probably. Most LMS problems come down to users, enrollments, course tracking and reports, which work the same way across platforms. We'll tell you honestly on the free call.
