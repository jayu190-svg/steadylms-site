Questions

What happens on the free call?

We introduce ourselves, then ask about your platform, your learners, what's working and what's frustrating. We don't need access to your LMS for this call. Within one business day you'll get a short written summary and our honest recommendation, even if that's "you don't need us".

Why is the audit paid?

A real audit means logging in, running reports and testing courses, which takes several hours. Charging for it keeps it thorough. If you start a care plan within 30 days, the full $450 comes off your first month.

How do you access our LMS? Is our data safe?

You create named accounts for us with only the access the work needs, read-only for the audit. We use multi-factor sign-in, never shared passwords, keep learner data in your systems, and are happy to sign your NDA.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Care plans have a 3-month minimum so we can get your platform into good shape. After that they run month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

What if we need more hours one month?

We tell you before we go over. Extra hours are $110 an hour and only happen with your OK. If you keep needing more, we'll suggest the next plan up.

Our LMS isn't one you listed. Can you still help?

Probably. Most LMS problems come down to users, enrollments, course tracking and reports, which work in similar ways across platforms. We'll tell you honestly on the free call.
