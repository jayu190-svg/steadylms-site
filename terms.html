Terms of Service

Last updated: November 16, 2026

These terms apply when you buy a care plan, an LMS health audit or a custom project from Steady LMS ("we", "us"). Steady LMS is based in San Jose, California. If you sign a separate written agreement with us, that agreement wins wherever it differs from these terms.

1. Care plans

  • Each plan includes a set number of hours per month (Essentials up to 5, Standard up to 10, Pro up to 20) and the reply time shown on our website.
  • Plans are billed monthly in advance and renew automatically each month.
  • Unused hours don't carry over to the next month.
  • If a month needs more time, we ask you first. Approved extra hours are billed at $110 per hour.
  • Plans have a 3-month minimum. After that, either side can cancel with 30 days' written notice, by email to hello@steadylms.com.

2. LMS health audit

  • The audit is a one-time service priced at $450, paid before we start.
  • You give us read-only or limited access through a named account. We review your platform and deliver a written report with a prioritized fix list, usually within 5 business days of getting access.
  • If you start a care plan within 30 days of receiving the report, we credit the $450 toward your first month.

3. Custom projects

  • Each project has a written quote describing the work, the price and the expected timing.
  • Unless the quote says otherwise, 50% is due before work starts and 50% on delivery.
  • Changes to the agreed scope are quoted separately before we do them.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card or bank account number. Prices are in US dollars. Invoices not paid within 15 days of the due date may pause work until they're settled.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Give us the access the work needs, through named accounts for our team.
  • Tell us who at your organization may send requests and approve extra hours.
  • Keep your own backups and stay responsible for your LMS contracts and licenses with your vendors.

6. Confidentiality and data

We keep your information and your learners' information confidential, use it only to do the work you asked for, and don't copy learner data to personal devices. We're happy to sign your NDA or data agreement. When our work ends, we remove our access and return or delete anything of yours we hold.

7. Who owns the work

Once paid for, reports, configurations, scripts and documents we create specifically for you belong to you. We keep the right to reuse our general know-how and tools that aren't specific to you.

8. Our promise and its limits

We do the work carefully and professionally. If something we did isn't right, tell us within 30 days and we'll fix it at no charge. We aren't responsible for problems caused by your LMS vendor, other software, or changes made by others. To the extent the law allows, our total responsibility for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.

9. Other

These terms are governed by the laws of California. We may update these terms; changes apply from the next billing month, and we'll email active clients about any important change. Questions: hello@steadylms.com.
